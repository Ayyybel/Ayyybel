I made a new shelf because I didn't like any of the ones I saw online. It seems any furniture that looks out of the ordinary is either incredibly expensive, made incredibly cheap, or incredibly ugly. I ended up making something that costed a reasonably amount, appears incredibly cheap, and is reasonably ugly, but I like it because I made it. 

total cost:
fasteners - 9.78
more fasteners - 9.49
paint - 14.20
even more fasteners - 6.85
paint + fasteners - 15.45
fasteners - 5.52
tubes - 53.19

that adds up to.... $114.48
