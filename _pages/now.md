It's nice to have so much going on, but I still have a good amount of downtime that I would like to be spending on creative projects, though I haven't really had those juices flowing much recently.

I've started to figure out some more goals. For the band, I want to get good enough at bass to play a show, which seems doable. I want to find some more creative projects to work on.

Planing on eventually probably doing a masters and moving to NYC, but that's all far enough out that it's not a certainty by any means.
