###### Last Updated: 5.11.2026

I've been living in Seattle (UDistrict) for a while now (9ish months), and I'm really enjoying it. I quit my retail job at Uniqlo in favor of a hybrid remote job at an accounting firm. I'm still figuring the job out, but have settled in and gotten into a comfortable routine. My weeks have gotten pretty full, with near-daily scheduled activities. Mondays are movie nights, Tuesday is trivia every other week, Wednesday is board game night, Thursday is band practice (I'm starting a band!), every few Fridays is poker, and weekends are generally up in the air. Sprinkled in, I work out, fly fpv drones, and play video games. It's nice to have so much going on, but I still have a good amount of downtime that I would like to be spending on creative projects, though I haven't really had those juices flowing much recently.

I've started to figure out some more goals. For the band, I want to get good enough at bass to play a show, which seems doable. I want to find some more creative projects to work on. I recently finished a bookshelf made of sonotube, but haven't started anything since. 

Further down the line (2028) I am considering starting a masters program, probably an MBA with a focus in finance or economics.
