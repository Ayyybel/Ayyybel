    A page about what I’ve been doing lately. If you’ve never seen a now page before, check out nownownow

    Last Updated: 8.1.2026

    I’ve been living in Seattle (UDistrict) for almost a year now, and I’m really enjoying it, but am moving to Lower Queen Anne soon shuffling around Seattle and exploring different neighborhoods is fun. Still working at an accounting firm. My weeks have gotten pretty full, with near-daily scheduled activities. I’ve been staying quite busy, practicing a bunch for the band, playing games, seeing friends, and getting outside. Most weekends I’ve been leaving Seattle to go camping or see family or friends. Still working out a ton, but I haven’t been flying fpv drones much recently. It’s nice to have so much going on, but I still have a good amount of downtime that I would like to be spending on creative projects, though I haven’t really had those juices flowing much recently.

    I’ve started to figure out some more goals. For the band, I want to get good enough at bass to play a show, which seems doable. I want to find some more creative projects to work on.

    Planing on eventually probably doing a masters and moving to NYC, but that’s all far enough out that it’s not a certainty by any means.
